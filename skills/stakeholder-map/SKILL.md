# Stakeholder map

Projects rarely fail because of a missing task; they fail because someone important learned about them too late. This skill lists the people around the project, ranks them, and plans who hears what, when.

## When to use

- After `project-charter`, as soon as more than two people are involved.
- Before a change that will surprise someone: a reorganization, works in a shared building, a new tool for a team.
- Not to split the work itself: use `responsibility-matrix`.

## Inputs

1. The project charter, or the project in a few sentences.
2. Everyone the person running the project can think of who is affected, who decides, who pays, who can delay or block.

## Method

1. **List the stakeholders widely**, then group them: decision makers, people doing the work, people affected, people with a veto (authorities, landlords, legal, unions, co-owners), people who simply need to know.
2. **Place them on an influence and interest grid**: manage closely (high influence, high interest), keep satisfied (high influence, low interest), keep informed (low influence, high interest), monitor (low, low).
3. **For each key stakeholder**, write what they need, what they fear, what they could do if surprised, and their current position (supports, neutral, against).
4. **Plan the communication**: message, channel, frequency and messenger for each group. Bad news travels best early and in person.
5. **Prepare the hard conversations**: the two or three people likely to object, with their probable objections and an answer.
6. **Review the map** at each milestone: influence and positions change.

## Output

```markdown
### Stakeholder map: <project>
| Stakeholder | Group | Influence | Interest | Needs and fears | Position | Plan |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

### Communication plan
| Who | What | Channel | When | Messenger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
```

## Quality bar

- People with a veto (authorities, owners, legal) are on the map.
- Each key stakeholder has needs, fears and a position, not just a name.
- The communication plan names a messenger and a date for each group.
- Written in the user's language, with the terms below.
