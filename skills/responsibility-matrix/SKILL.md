# Responsibility matrix (RACI)

When everyone is responsible, nobody is. This skill gives each task and each decision exactly one accountable person, and shows who must be consulted or simply informed.

## When to use

- After `work-breakdown`, as soon as several people share the work.
- When tasks fall through the cracks or two people think they own the same decision.
- Not to map influence and communication: use `stakeholder-map`.

## Inputs

1. The task list or the main decisions of the project.
2. The people involved and their availability.

## Method

1. **List the rows**: the main tasks and, separately, the main decisions (budget, choice of supplier, go-live date).
2. **List the columns**: the people or roles involved.
3. **Fill the matrix**: R (responsible: does the work), A (accountable: approves and answers for the result), C (consulted before: two-way), I (informed after: one-way).
4. **Apply the rules**: exactly one A per row; at least one R per row; few C, because each C slows things down; the A can also be the R on small projects.
5. **Check the matrix**: rows without A or R (gaps), people with too many R (overload), people with only I on things they care about (future conflict).
6. **Share it** and confirm it with each person; update it when someone joins or leaves.

## Output

```markdown
### RACI: <project> (version <n>, <date>)
| Task or decision | Person 1 | Person 2 | Person 3 | Person 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| … | A, R | C | I | – |

Gaps: … | Overloads: … | To confirm with: …
```

## Quality bar

- Every row has exactly one A and at least one R.
- Decisions are listed, not only tasks.
- Overloads and gaps are named, with a fix.
- Written in the user's language, with the terms below.
