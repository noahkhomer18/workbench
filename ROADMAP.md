# Roadmap

Skills, countries and languages we'd love to add. Each one is a good first contribution: pick one, open an issue saying you're on it, and follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Projects people run

- [ ] `event-planning`: plan an event from the budget to the run sheet of the day
- [ ] `house-move`: a move with its checklist, paperwork and dates
- [ ] `home-renovation`: a renovation from the first quotes to the final inspection
- [ ] `fundraising-campaign`: a campaign for a nonprofit or a school, from goal to thank-you notes

## Methods

- [ ] `kanban-board`: make the work visible and limit work in progress
- [ ] `capacity-planning`: who can do what and when, without overload
- [ ] `project-portfolio`: choose between several projects with limited time and money
- [ ] `sprint-planning`: plan and review work in short iterations
- [ ] `contract-checklist`: the questions to ask before signing a service contract (not legal advice)
- [ ] `remote-team-rituals`: a working rhythm for a distributed team
